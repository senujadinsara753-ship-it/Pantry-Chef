# Features -
Mention what in your pantry (Food Making Items)
Diet or preferences (Vegetarian,Spicy,No Nuts & Etc.)
Choose the maximum time in minute to prepare the meal 
Mention how many servings 
Then click the Suggested Recipes - Number of Recipes will Appear
Click Meal Plan - Weekly meal plan will Appear
Click Shopping - When add the recipes to your plan to build a shopping list.
